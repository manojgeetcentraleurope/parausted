# Privacy Policy — Outline (ParaUsted)

> ⚠️ This is an OUTLINE. Must be reviewed by a Spanish lawyer before launch.

## Required Sections (GDPR Art. 13 + LOPDGDD)

1. **Data Controller** — ParaUsted legal entity, address, email, DPO contact
2. **What Data We Collect**
   - Merchants: email, name, business info, IBAN, Bizum phone
   - Buyers: email (receipt), phone (optional, delivery)
   - Recipients: email/phone (delivery only)
3. **Why We Collect It (Legal Basis)**
   - Contract performance (merchant account, gift card delivery)
   - Legitimate interest (platform operation, analytics)
   - Consent (marketing, if any)
4. **How Long We Keep It**
   - Buyer/recipient PII: auto-deleted 30 days after voucher expiry/redemption
   - Financial records: 5 years (Spanish tax law)
   - Audit events: indefinite (immutable, anonymized)
5. **Who We Share It With**
   - Supabase (database hosting, EU Frankfurt)
   - Stripe (payment processing)
   - Resend (email delivery)
   - Meta (WhatsApp delivery)
   - Sentry (error tracking — no PII sent)
6. **Your Rights (ARSULIPO)**
   - Access, Rectification, Suppression, Limitation, Portability, Opposition
   - How to exercise: email to privacidad@parausted.es
   - Response within 1 month
7. **Data Blocking (Spain-specific)**
   - On erasure: data "blocked" for legal liabilities period, then destroyed
8. **Cookies**
   - Essential only (session). No tracking cookies without consent.
   - Reference to Cookie Policy
9. **International Transfers**
   - All data stored in EU (Supabase EU Frankfurt)
   - Stripe: EU data processing
10. **Changes to Policy**
    - Notify users of material changes
11. **Supervisory Authority**
    - AEPD (Agencia Española de Protección de Datos)
    - Website: aepd.es
