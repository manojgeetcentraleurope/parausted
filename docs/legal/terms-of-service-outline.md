# Terms of Service — Outline (ParaUsted)

> ⚠️ This is an OUTLINE. Must be reviewed by a Spanish lawyer before launch.

## Sections Required

1. **Definitions** — Platform, Merchant, Customer, Recipient, Gift Card, Voucher
2. **Service Description** — What ParaUsted does and does not do
3. **Merchant Obligations** — Deliver service, maintain quality, honor vouchers
4. **Customer Rights** — Refund policy, withdrawal right, personalization clause
5. **Payment Terms** — 5% commission on online, FREE for offline, payout schedule (85/15)
6. **Gift Card Validity** — Minimum 12 months (Spanish consumer law)
7. **Personalization & Withdrawal Right** — EU Directive 2011/83 Art. 16 exemption
8. **Payment Disclaimer** — ParaUsted not responsible for direct payments (Bizum/cash/bank)
9. **Intellectual Property** — ParaUsted brand, merchant content
10. **Data Protection** — Reference to Privacy Policy, GDPR/LOPDGDD compliance
11. **Liability Limitations** — Platform liability caps
12. **Termination** — How merchant/platform can terminate
13. **Governing Law** — Spanish law, courts of Seville
14. **Contact** — Legal entity, address, email
